# EconByte 1.1.4 — content accuracy audit (Phase 12)

Date: 2026-09-14 · Branch `claude/econbyte-114-pro` · Owner order: "drag a fine tooth comb through all of the new content".

## Scope

Everything added since 1.1.3 shipped, plus the two 1.1.3 packs:

- `EconByte/Resources/courses-v1.json`: 3 courses × 6 lessons. Every block was read (paragraphs, callouts, key terms, diagram captions against what `DiagramView.swift` draws, chart titles, captions, markers and the synthetic data, quiz questions, correct answers, distractors, explanations, takeaways, sources).
- `EconByte/Resources/packs-v1.json`: 6 packs × 4 topics × 8 cards = 192 cards, plus pack and topic summaries. Checked title, definition, example, `claim` metadata and source.
- `EconByte/Resources/brief-sample.json`: every figure re-read against the official BLS, Federal Reserve and BEA pages.
- In-app copy that makes factual claims: Pro paywall, Unlock All paywall, pack rows, lesson lock, Pro tab, Home course row, brief teaser and banner, the Settings "Sources & editorial policy" page, and the `EconByte.storekit` product descriptions.

## Method

1. Each factual claim was checked against the page it cites, opened on 2026-09-14 (WebFetch; `curl` with a browser user agent for loc.gov). Where the page could not support the claim, the text was corrected to what the page says, or the claim was re-cited to another page on the approved host list.
2. Numbers, signs, units, periods, seasonal-adjustment notes, dates, names, definitions and causal wording were compared one by one. Every `observation` claim was checked as a reading reproducible from its page. Every `illustration` claim's arithmetic was recomputed.
3. Every quiz was re-solved independently. A distractor that is arguably correct counts as a defect (`ambiguous`).
4. Charts were checked in two ways:
   - A script confirmed that all 19 charts have a `dataNote` starting "Synthetic", that every candle is valid (high ≥ max(open, close), low ≤ min(open, close)), that x values increase, and that every marker sits on a data point.
   - Each chart was also checked by hand to confirm it shows the pattern its lesson names.
5. Tone: educational, with no advice, predictions or named securities, and balanced where evidence is contested. Also checked: no stale wording, no year after 2026, US spelling, plain English, and no duplicates of the 120 core cards.
6. The work was split across sub-agents by pack and by course, each writing a findings file with exact old → new edits. The phase lead reviewed every proposed edit and re-checked the highest-impact numbers personally. Edits were applied with an old-value check and in the files' own JSON format (minimal diff). `scripts/validate_content.mjs` (packs, courses, brief) and the `EconByteTests` suite were then re-run.

Severity key:
- `factual`: wrong number, date, name, attribution or mechanism.
- `outdated`: superseded vintage.
- `ambiguous`: imprecise enough to mislead, or a quiz with more than one defensible answer.
- `unsourced`: the claim is not supported by the cited page.
- `tone`: advice, prediction or loaded framing.
- `typo` / `spelling`: includes British spellings.
- `duplicate`: repeats a core or other pack card.

In the tables, "correct" means the item was checked and needed no change.

# EconByte 1.1.4 copy trim (2026-09-15, Lane A2)

Order: "remove non-essential strings app-wide (explanatory subtitles, repeated disclaimers — keep one
'Educational content, not financial advice' at lesson end, brief and paywall)".

What stays, once each:
- **Lesson end:** `EducationalNoticeBanner("Educational content, not financial advice.")` after the sources
  (`LessonView`, id `educationalNotice`).
- **Card session end:** the same line on the session-complete screen (`SessionCompleteView`, id `sessionNotAdvice`) —
  the end of a card "lesson".
- **Brief:** the brief document's own `disclaimer` field, at the end of every brief (`BriefDocumentView`).
- **Paywall:** `PlanCopy.notAdvice` under the auto-renew disclosure (`ProPaywallContent`, id `proPaywallNotAdvice`).
- Legal copy App Review requires is not "non-essential" and was kept or tightened, never dropped: the auto-renew
  disclosure, the complete trial line, Terms of Use / Privacy Policy, Restore, the analytics consent primer, the
  Sources & editorial policy sheet.
